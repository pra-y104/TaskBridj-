How it works

1. A business creates a campaign

Task type, instructions, participant requirements, questions, evidence and reward per approved participant.

2. Participants complete the task

Participants accept an available task, follow the instructions, answer the questions and upload the required evidence.

3. Reviewed submissions earn

The business or a reviewer approves or rejects with a reason. Approved submissions record earnings for the participant.
