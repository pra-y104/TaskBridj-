Privacy Policy

This policy explains how TaskBridj collects and uses personal data. We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable law.

1. Data we collect

Account data: name, email address, role and password (stored securely, never in readable form). Profile data: country, language, device, interests, and for businesses the organisation name, industry, website and contact email. Task data: your answers, uploaded evidence files, proof codes and file fingerprints used to detect duplicates. Payment data: bank name, account name and account number when you request a withdrawal; we do not store card details. Technical data: basic logs needed to run and secure the service.

2. How we use it

To create and run your account, match participants with projects, process submissions, pay rewards and refunds, prevent fraud and abuse, resolve disputes and appeals, comply with the law, and improve the service.

3. Who sees your data

A project creator sees the answers and evidence you submit for their project, but not your email address or bank details. TaskBridj administrators can see data needed to run the platform. Evidence files are stored privately and are visible only to you, the project creator and authorised administrators. We use service providers to host and operate TaskBridj (for example database and web hosting) and, when enabled, a payment provider to process payments. They may only use data to provide those services.

4. Sensitive material

Do not upload anything that is not needed for the task. If a task needs recordings of your screen, audio or video, the project description must say so before you accept it. Remove personal information that is not required before you upload evidence.

5. Retention and security

We keep data while your account is active and as needed for financial records, disputes and legal duties, then delete or anonymise it. We use access controls, encrypted connections and role-based permissions, but no system is completely secure.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where we rely on it. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Contact us at [your support email].

7. Children and changes

TaskBridj is for people aged 18 and over. We will post any change to this policy on this page.
