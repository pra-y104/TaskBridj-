Help center

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Contact support below.

How do I start earning?

Create a participant account, complete your profile, open Tasks, and take a task that fits you. Read the instructions, do the work, add your proof code and evidence, and submit. You are paid when the work is approved. Earnings depend on approved submissions and no income is guaranteed.

How long do I have to finish a task?

After you take a task you have a limited time to submit it (currently 24 hours). If the time runs out, the task goes back to other participants and cannot be submitted late.

What is the proof code?

Every task you take has a personal code. It must appear in your screenshot or recording and you type it when you submit. This stops copied or reused evidence.

Why was my submission rejected?

The business must give a written reason. If you think it is wrong, tap Appeal on the task within the appeal period (currently 3 days). TaskBridj reviews appeals and pays you if the work was valid.

How long does a review take?

Businesses are asked to review within about 5 days. If a submission is not reviewed in time, it can be approved automatically unless it is flagged for a quality or fraud check.

How do I withdraw my earnings?

Open Wallet, enter your bank details and the amount. You can withdraw once your available balance reaches the minimum shown in your Wallet. Any fee is shown before you confirm.

How is a project priced?

Choose a task type and the number of participants. TaskBridj calculates the price on its servers, applies volume discounts and any screen-recording extra, and shows the total before you confirm. You are charged for approved work only.

When does my project go live?

After you submit it, TaskBridj confirms funding and reviews the project. It then appears to participants who match your country, device and experience settings.

Can I change a project after submitting?

You can edit the title, description, requirements and reference link, and pause or resume recruitment. Price, number of participants and questions are locked so funded budgets stay safe.

How do refunds work?

After your project deadline you can request a refund of the unused amount, minus a handling fee that is shown before you confirm. Money for approved or pending work is not refundable.

What is not allowed?

Fake or reused evidence, multiple accounts, bots, and tasks asking for paid likes, follows, views, fake reviews, stolen logins or personal data. Accounts that break these rules can be suspended.
